ATMIK YOGA & FITNESS - GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE SETUP
===================================================
Site: https://atmik-yoga-fitness.github.io/

STEP 1 - UPLOAD TO GITHUB
 1. Unzip this folder on your computer.
 2. Open your repo "atmik-yoga-fitness.github.io" on github.com.
 3. Click Add file > Upload files. Drag ALL files from the unzipped
    folder (not the folder itself). Replace the old index.html.
    Include the hidden file ".nojekyll" if your computer shows it
    (it is optional).
 4. Click "Commit changes". Wait 1-2 minutes.
 5. Test in your browser:
      /sitemap.xml   /robots.txt   /favicon.ico   /og-image.jpg

STEP 2 - ADD THE SITE IN SEARCH CONSOLE
 1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in
    (use the Google account that owns the Business Profile).
 2. Click "Add property" > choose "URL prefix" (the right box).
 3. Type exactly: https://atmik-yoga-fitness.github.io/
    (GitHub Pages does not allow the "Domain" option.)

STEP 3 - VERIFY OWNERSHIP (pick ONE method)
 Method A: HTML tag (easiest)
  1. In the verify screen choose "HTML tag". Copy the line like:
     <meta name="google-site-verification" content="XXXX">
  2. Open index.html on GitHub (pencil icon), paste that line right
     after <head> (before <meta charset>), commit.
  3. Go back to Search Console and click Verify.
 Method B: HTML file
  1. Download the file Google gives (googleXXXX.html).
  2. Upload it to the repo root (same place as index.html), commit,
     then click Verify.
 Keep the tag/file there forever or you lose verification.

STEP 4 - SUBMIT THE SITEMAP
 1. Left menu > Sitemaps.
 2. In "Add a new sitemap" type: sitemap.xml  and press Submit.
 3. Status should say "Success". (It may say "Couldn't fetch" for a
    few hours at first; wait and refresh.)

STEP 5 - ASK GOOGLE TO INDEX THE HOME PAGE
 1. Top search bar > paste https://atmik-yoga-fitness.github.io/
 2. Press Enter, then click "Request indexing". Wait for the result.
 3. Indexing can take from a few days to 2-3 weeks.

STEP 6 - CHECK THESE LATER
 - Pages: should show your page as "Indexed".
 - Performance: shows what people search to find you.
 - Experience > Core Web Vitals / Mobile usability: look for issues.
 - Re-run "Request indexing" after big changes to index.html.

STEP 7 - GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE (separate, important for the map card)
 1. Go to business.google.com, open Atmik Yoga & Fitness.
 2. Edit profile > Website: set https://atmik-yoga-fitness.github.io/
 3. Photos: upload logo (atmik-logo) and the banner, plus real photos
    of the studio and classes. Set the logo and cover photo.
 4. Check Name, Address, Phone, Hours, Category (Yoga studio) and
    Services are complete. Ask students for reviews using the QR.
 5. Link Search Console to Business Profile is not needed.

STEP 8 - REFRESH SOCIAL PREVIEWS
 - Facebook Sharing Debugger: https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
   paste your link, click "Scrape Again".
 - WhatsApp caches previews; share the link in a new chat to test.

NOTES
 - The globe icon on Google results is replaced after Google
   re-crawls the page; this can take days to weeks.
 - Nothing here guarantees a ranking; consistent reviews, photos
   and a complete Business Profile help the most.
 - The Search Console verification code can only come from your
   own Google account, so it cannot be pre-filled in this zip.
